# Bonus: 10 animation prompts (Content Engine)

Paste any prompt into Claude. It builds the animation as an artifact you can watch and tweak. Swap the words in [brackets] for your business, then add: "make it loop smoothly and fill a 9:16 vertical frame".

**To use one in a reel:** play it full screen and screen-record it (phone or computer), then post it as a b-roll clip, or put it before or after a reel-maker video in any editor (CapCut, Instagram's own editor).

## 01 Map Route
Build an animated HTML canvas: a dark city map seen from above, slightly rotated, with rounded block shapes. A glowing ice-blue route draws itself from a start pin to a red end pin with right-angle turns, and a white dot travels along it. Add a bottom card: '[Delivery · 12 min]' and '[3.4 km · fastest route]'. Loop every 2 seconds.

## 02 Headline
Build an animated newspaper front page called '[Your Shop Times]'. The headline '[A café let AI answer its phones.]' types in letter by letter in a bold serif font, then a highlighter swipes across a subhead '[Bookings up 3x in a week]'. Grey lines stand in for body text. The page slowly zooms and tilts slightly.

## 03 Teardown
Build an exploded-view animation of [a phone]: 5 isometric layers ([glass, display, battery, board, back]) stacked, then smoothly separating vertically. After they separate, thin lines and labels appear next to each layer. Dark background, clean flat colours.

## 04 Stat Chart
Build a dark dashboard card animation: the label '[Monthly revenue]', a big number counting up from 0 to [$48,200], a green '▲ [312]%' badge, and a glowing exponential line chart drawing left to right with a soft gradient fill under it and a white dot at the tip.

## 05 Globe Arcs
Build a rotating wireframe globe on a near-black background. From [Kuala Lumpur], glowing arcs fly out one by one to [Singapore, Tokyo, London, Sydney, New York, Dubai, Hong Kong]. Add a caption '[Kuala Lumpur → 7 markets]'. Smooth, slow rotation, cyan glow.

## 06 Agent Swarm
Build an animation of a grid of 40 small robot tiles (rounded squares with two dot eyes) connected by faint lines. Tiles light up in waves and glowing dots travel along the lines between them, like agents passing work. Caption counts up: '[40 agents working]'.

## 07 Paper Desk
Build a dark desk scene: two cream paper sheets ('[SOP v1]' and '[STEP 3]') slide in and land at slight angles with soft shadows. A red pen circle draws itself around a paragraph with a handwritten '[fix this]', then a yellow sticky note saying '[by Friday]' pops onto the corner.

## 08 Iso Flow
Build an isometric flow diagram: 4 blocks rise up in sequence and are labelled [Comment → DM → Follow → Guide]. Glowing particles flow from block to block along the path in a loop. Ice-blue and teal blocks, last block white, dark background.

## 09 Ink Wash
Build a Japanese ink-wash (sumi-e) landscape animation on cream paper: a red sun fades in, then three mountain ridges brush in from left to right, light grey to near black, and a small bird flaps across the sky. Add a red stamp-style character in the corner.

## 10 Hologram
Build a hologram animation: a teal projector base with a light cone rising from it, and a glowing cyan wireframe [drone] slowly rotating and hovering inside the beam with flicker and scanlines. Add a small mono label '[SCAN · 98%]' in the corner.

## Tips
- Say "slower", "more glow" or "use my brand colours [#hex]" to adjust.
- Ask for "a version with a transparent background" to put it over your own video.
